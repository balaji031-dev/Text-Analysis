Off-Page SEO

If I reach out and ask someone to link to me, that’s off-page SEO. Why? Because I didn’t change anything about my website during that process.
- Off-page SEO will help make your website popular on the internet, so you can get more visibility. With the on-page techniques, we can get visibility in search engines. However, off-page SEO techniques will help improve your website position in Search Engine Results Pages (SERP).
- If I improve my page speed by optimizing some images, then that’s on-page SEO because I made that change directly on my website.
